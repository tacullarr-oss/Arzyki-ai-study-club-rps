How to Play

  1. Click Start Camera to activate your webcam and load the AI model.
  2. Hold up a gesture in front of your camera:
    • Fist: Rock
    • Open Palm: Paper
    • Peace Sign: Scissors
  3. Watch the live Detected label update in real-time.
  4. Click Play Hand to submit your gesture against the computer!
  5. Want to use your own custom model? Click Use Custom Model, paste your Teachable Machine shareable URL, and click load!
